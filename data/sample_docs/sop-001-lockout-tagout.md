# SOP-001 Lockout/Tagout for Equipment Servicing

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Scope

This procedure applies to any servicing where unexpected energization, start-up or release of stored
energy could injure a technician. It covers electrical, pneumatic, vacuum and thermal energy sources.

## Required Equipment

- A personal padlock with a unique key, issued by the maintenance supervisor.
- A red DANGER tag completed with name, date and reason.
- A voltage tester verified on a known live source before and after use.

## Procedure

1. Notify affected operators that the equipment will be shut down.
2. Stop the equipment using normal controls.
3. Isolate all energy sources: open the main disconnect, close and bleed pneumatic supply valves, and
   vent vacuum chambers to atmosphere.
4. Apply your personal lock and tag to each isolation point. Each technician applies their own lock.
5. Release stored energy: discharge capacitors, allow hot parts to cool, and block any raised axes.
6. Verify zero energy by attempting a normal start and by testing for voltage at the load side.
7. Perform the service work.

## Removing Locks

Only the technician who applied a lock may remove it. If that person is unavailable, the maintenance
supervisor may remove the lock only after contacting the technician and completing form LOTO-7.
