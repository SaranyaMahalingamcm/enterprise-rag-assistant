# Safety Bulletin 2026-03 LC-500 Door Interlock Inspection

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Summary

Some LC-500 units built before serial number LC5-04000 may have an interlock actuator bracket that
loosens over time. A loose bracket can cause nuisance E101 alarms and, in rare cases, delay detection
of the door opening.

## Required Action

Inspect the door interlock actuator bracket on every affected unit within 30 days. Apply lockout/tagout
per SOP-001, then check that both bracket screws are tight and that the actuator enters the switch
without contacting the housing. Replace the bracket with kit AFT-LC-IK3 if it is cracked.

## Prohibited Practices

Never bypass, tape over or jumper the door interlock, even temporarily for troubleshooting. Operating
the LC-500 with a defeated interlock exposes people to invisible infrared laser radiation.
