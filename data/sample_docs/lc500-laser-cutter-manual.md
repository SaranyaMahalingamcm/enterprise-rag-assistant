# LC-500 Laser Cutter Operator and Maintenance Manual

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools). Not a real product manual.

## Overview

The LC-500 is a 500 W enclosed CO2 laser cutter for sheet acrylic, wood and thin anodized aluminum.
The working area is 1200 mm x 900 mm. The enclosure door is fitted with a dual-channel safety
interlock; the laser cannot fire while the door is open.

## Daily Startup Procedure

1. Confirm the CH-40 chiller is running and the coolant temperature reads between 18 and 22 degrees Celsius.
2. Switch on the fume extraction unit and wait until the airflow indicator is green.
3. Turn the key switch to ON and allow the controller to complete its self-test (about 45 seconds).
4. Home the gantry from the control panel before loading material.
5. Verify that the air assist pressure is between 1.5 and 2.0 bar.

## Focus Lens Cleaning

Clean the focus lens every 8 operating hours, or immediately if cut quality drops.
Power down the laser and wait 5 minutes for the lens holder to cool. Remove the lens holder with the
supplied tool, then wipe the lens with lint-free optical tissue moistened with reagent-grade
isopropyl alcohol, using a single stroke from center to edge. Never reuse a tissue. Inspect the lens
under a light; replace it (part AFT-LC-LENS-2) if pitting or a brown burn mark is visible.

## Mirror Alignment

Mirror alignment should be checked monthly or after the machine has been moved. Use the target
card method described in SOP-014. Misalignment usually shows up as uneven cut depth across the bed.

## Common Alarms

- **E101 Door interlock open**: close the enclosure door fully; check the interlock switch actuator for damage.
- **E104 Coolant flow low**: check the CH-40 chiller reservoir level and the return hose for kinks.
- **E110 Laser tube over-temperature**: stop the job and allow the tube to cool for 15 minutes; confirm chiller setpoint.
