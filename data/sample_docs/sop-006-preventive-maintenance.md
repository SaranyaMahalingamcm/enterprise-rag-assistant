# SOP-006 Preventive Maintenance Schedule

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Purpose

This SOP defines the minimum preventive maintenance (PM) intervals for Acme Fab Tools equipment.
Intervals may be shortened by the site maintenance lead, but never extended without an approved
deviation record.

## PM Intervals

| Equipment | Task | Interval |
|-----------|------|----------|
| LC-500 | Clean focus lens | Every 8 operating hours |
| LC-500 | Check mirror alignment | Monthly |
| CM-220 | Backlash measurement | Every 500 operating hours |
| CM-220 | Replace pull studs | Every 2,000 tool changes |
| VC-90 | Replace lid O-ring | Every 6 months |
| VC-90 | Scroll pump tip seal replacement | Every 12 months |
| CH-40 | Clean condenser filter | Every 2 weeks |
| CH-40 | Replace coolant | Every 6 months |
| RA-6 | TCP calibration verification | Quarterly |

## Recording PM Work

Record every completed PM task in the maintenance log with the equipment serial number, the task, the
technician initials and any measured values. Overdue PM tasks are reported in the weekly
reliability review.
