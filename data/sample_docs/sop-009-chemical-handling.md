# SOP-009 Chemical Handling for Maintenance

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Covered Chemicals

- Isopropyl alcohol (IPA), used for optics and surface cleaning.
- AFT-COOL corrosion inhibitor, used in chiller coolant.
- Water-soluble machining coolant concentrate, used in the CM-220.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Wear nitrile gloves and safety glasses when handling any covered chemical. Add a face shield when
decanting more than 1 liter. Do not wear latex gloves with IPA because they degrade quickly.

## Storage

Store IPA in a flammable-liquids cabinet, in containers of 1 liter or less at the workstation.
Keep AFT-COOL concentrate away from strong acids. Label every secondary container with the chemical
name and hazard pictogram.

## Spills

For spills under 500 ml, absorb with the yellow spill kit pads, place the pads in a sealed bag and
dispose of them as chemical waste. For larger spills, evacuate the area and call the site emergency
line. Always consult the safety data sheet (SDS) before cleaning an unfamiliar spill.
