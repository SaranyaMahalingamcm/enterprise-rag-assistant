# VC-90 Vacuum Coating Chamber Manual

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Overview

The VC-90 is a benchtop magnetron sputter coater used to apply thin decorative and conductive films.
It uses a two-stage vacuum system: a dry scroll roughing pump and a turbomolecular pump.

## Pump-Down Sequence

1. Close and latch the chamber lid; confirm the O-ring is seated and free of debris.
2. Start the roughing pump and wait until chamber pressure falls below 5 x 10^-2 mbar.
3. Start the turbomolecular pump. It reaches full speed in about 6 minutes.
4. The chamber is ready for coating when base pressure is below 5 x 10^-5 mbar.

A normal pump-down to base pressure takes less than 25 minutes. Longer pump-down times usually
indicate a leak or a contaminated chamber; see the vacuum leak troubleshooting guide (TS-003).

## Sputter Target Replacement

Replace the sputter target when erosion depth exceeds 70 percent of target thickness. Vent the
chamber with dry nitrogen, disconnect the DC power supply, and apply lockout/tagout per SOP-001
before removing the target clamp ring. Torque the clamp ring screws to 1.2 N m in a star pattern.

## O-Ring Care

Inspect the lid O-ring at every venting. Wipe with a lint-free cloth only; do not apply vacuum grease
unless the O-ring is Viton and the grease is a low-vapor-pressure type. Replace the O-ring
(part AFT-VC-OR90) every 6 months.
