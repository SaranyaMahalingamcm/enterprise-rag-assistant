## Service Tiers

- **Tier 1**: operator-level fixes documented in the manuals, such as clearing interlock alarms or
  refilling consumables. No ticket required, but log the event.
- **Tier 2**: on-site maintenance technician. Open a service request in the maintenance system with
  the alarm code, equipment serial number and steps already taken. Target response is 4 business hours.
- **Tier 3**: Acme Fab Tools field service engineer. Requested by the Tier 2 technician when a
  component must be replaced under warranty. Target response is 2 business days.
