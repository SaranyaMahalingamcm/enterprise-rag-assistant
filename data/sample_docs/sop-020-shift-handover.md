# SOP-020 Maintenance Shift Handover

> Synthetic document for a fictional manufacturer (Acme Fab Tools).

## Purpose

A structured shift handover prevents lost information about equipment that is down, locked out or
running with a temporary workaround.

## Handover Checklist

1. List every piece of equipment currently under lockout/tagout, with the lock owner's name.
2. List open Tier 2 and Tier 3 service requests and their current status.
3. Report any alarm that occurred three or more times during the shift.
4. Report temporary compensations, such as backlash values entered in CM-220 parameter P-1851.
5. Confirm chemical waste containers are closed and labeled.

## Handover Meeting

The outgoing and incoming technicians review the checklist face to face for at least 10 minutes. The
incoming technician signs the handover log to accept responsibility. Locks are never transferred
between technicians; the incoming technician applies their own lock before the outgoing technician
removes theirs.
